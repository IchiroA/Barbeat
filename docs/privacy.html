Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Barbeat collects no data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Sound and screen frames are analyzed live on your Mac and never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The only network traffic goes to lights you set up yourself, on your own network, and a once-a-day check for a newer version on GitHub (which you can turn off).

Sound

To animate the menu bar, Barbeat listens to what your Mac plays (all of it, or one app you choose) or to an audio input you pick, such as a microphone. The sound is turned into levels for the bars as it plays and then discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent. macOS asks your permission first, and you can turn it off anytime under System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Screen

If you choose Screen colors, Barbeat reads small frames of your screen (192 pixels wide, a few to 15 a second) to work out their colors, or only the window of the app you're listening to. Each frame is analyzed in memory and thrown away; none are saved or sent. Barbeat's own windows are left out. macOS asks your permission first and shows its recording icon in the menu bar while this is on.

Voice

If you turn on Voice, Barbeat listens for singing or speech in the same sound, using Apple's sound recognizer built into macOS. It runs on your Mac; nothing is recorded, saved or sent, and Barbeat never turns speech into words.

Scenes

Scenes that switch on their own check, every two seconds, which apps are playing sound and whether any app is using a microphone. Barbeat only reads that yes or no from macOS; it never listens to the microphone for this. Nothing about it is saved or sent.

Desktop picture

The Wallpaper colors and the "From Wallpaper" button read your current desktop picture on your Mac to pick a few colors. The picture isn't copied, saved or sent.

Lights

If you set up lights, Barbeat talks to your Philips Hue Bridge and Art-Net devices (such as WLED) directly over your local network. To find a Hue Bridge, it looks on your local network and may also ask Philips' public discovery service (discovery.meethue.com), which lists bridges registered from your internet connection. That request includes no information about you beyond what any web request does (your IP address); Philips' own privacy policy covers it. macOS asks for Local Network permission the first time.

Updates

About once a day Barbeat asks GitHub (api.github.com) for its latest release, to tell you when there's a newer version. Like any web request, this shows GitHub your IP address; it sends nothing else about you, and GitHub's own privacy policy covers it. If you choose to update, Barbeat downloads the new version from GitHub and installs it only if it's signed by its developer and notarized by Apple. You can turn the daily check off in the update dialog.

What's stored on your Mac

  • Your settings, in macOS's preferences for Barbeat.
  • Hue pairing keys, in your login keychain.
  • While Barbeat controls Hue lights: a note of how the lights were set, in Barbeat's Application Support folder, so they can be put back even after a crash. It's removed once they're restored.

Nothing is stored anywhere else, and Barbeat has no server of its own.

Children

Barbeat collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
