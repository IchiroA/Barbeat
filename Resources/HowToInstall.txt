Barbeat
=======

Turns your Mac's menu bar into a light show that moves with whatever you're playing.

Made by Ichiro Ashihara (@IchiroA). © 2026 Ichiro Ashihara. Open source under the MIT License.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later.

Install
1. Drag "Barbeat" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. The first time, macOS checks that you want to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click Open.
   A small bar-chart icon appears in the menu bar (there's no Dock icon).
3. macOS asks to let Barbeat record system audio. Click Allow.
   It only listens so it can animate the menu bar. Nothing is recorded or saved.
4. macOS also asks to let Barbeat record the screen. Allow it to have the colors follow what's on screen
   (if macOS offers "Quit & Reopen", click it). Barbeat only reads a few tiny frames a second for their
   colors; nothing is recorded or saved. Without it, the colors use your Mac's accent color, or pick
   any other palette in the menu bar panel.
5. Play some music. It starts with stereo Center bars in your screen's colors and a glow around the screen edges.

If macOS won't open the app
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Barbeat.

Settings
Click the bar-chart icon in the menu bar to pick a style (LED, Bars, Center, Glow, Pulse, VU, Aura,
Waterfall, Scope, Sparks), colors (Screen colors follow what's on screen; or make your own under Custom),
color drift, Stereo or full-width layout, brightness, Low/Mid/High boost, spark power, the screen edge glow
(and how far it reaches into the screen), Keep menu bar text readable, whether to show over full-screen
apps (just the edge glow, or the menu bar visuals too), and Launch at login.

Listen to: everything your Mac plays, just one app (say Spotify, so calls and alerts don't move the bars),
or a microphone / audio input. The first time you pick an input, macOS asks for microphone access.

If nothing moves
Make sure Barbeat is allowed under System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Screen & System Audio Recording, then click "Try Again" in the menu bar panel.
